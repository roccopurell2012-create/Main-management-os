# Proposed operating settings — owner decisions required

These are engineering recommendations, **not approved policy, configured jobs, legal retention periods or customer promises**. Business-record deletion is not automated. No production data has been changed. South African legal/privacy review must determine applicable retention/holds. All acceptance decisions are **To be confirmed**.

| Area | Recommended starting proposal | What exists / input needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sessions / abuse counters | Keep existing 12-hour sessions and daily expired-state maintenance | Current code supports this. A trusted daily schedule and maintenance credential are still needed; it is not running. |
| Account/company closure | Verify authority, suspend company access immediately, offer an approved private export, then archive. Propose a 30-day recovery window before considering reviewed erasure | Suspension/archive exist. Export and erasure are manual/reviewed work; agree the window and legal holds before promising anything. Revoke only relevant memberships; users can belong to other companies. Do not globally disable a multi-company user merely because one company closes. |
| Company business records / future invoices | Keep only for a company-approved purpose and period, with applicable statutory/contractual holds assessed professionally | No arbitrary fixed legal period is assumed. Approve a record-specific schedule before enabling business record modules or deletion. Invoice records may never collect payments. |
| Resolved support tickets | Propose 12 months after resolution, with legal/security holds handled separately | Decision required; no business-data purge job exists. Confirm whether longer troubleshooting history is needed. |
| Support attachments / superseded logos | Propose 90 days after ticket resolution or logo replacement, unless needed for an active issue/hold | Decide with the company; no automated deletion exists. Files are stored in the database and its backups, so coordinated retention and restore handling are necessary. |
| Notifications | Propose 90 days | Decision and safe purge implementation required. Current records persist. |
| Audit events | Propose 12 months with restricted access | Decision and reviewed purge required. Keep minimal event metadata, not message bodies or credentials. |
| Provider request/error logs | Propose 30 days, minimum fields/access needed for operations | Actual provider/settings/location To be confirmed; redact credentials and personal payloads. |
| Backups | Encrypted daily backups with a proposed 30-day rolling retention; provider-managed PITR with a proposed 7-day window if supported. Restrict operators; include bytea files; restore-test before launch and quarterly thereafter | Provider, regions, storage budget, maintenance responsibility and actual recovery capabilities To be confirmed. A pg_dump alone is not PITR. No backup or restore has been verified. Choose an acceptable measured data-loss window and recovery target; no recovery guarantee is promised. |
| File scanning | Keep 2 MB PNG/JPEG/PDF allowlist and private authenticated downloads. Before broader uploads, add a private quarantine/scan workflow; release files only on a clean result; deny on error/unknown result | Only signature/type/size checks currently exist. Choose a scanner and region or keep uploads unavailable for the launch scope until scanning is implemented. Do not send customer files to a public scanning service without a reviewed processing basis/contract. No scan or quarantine protection is currently claimed. |
| Regions | Prefer a provider-supported region appropriate for South African users and keep the application near its database; verify primary, replicas, backups and logs separately | Do not assume South African storage. Owner must confirm provider/regions and any cross-border arrangements. |

Technical sources checked 14 November 2026: [PostgreSQL PITR documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/continuous-archiving.html) and [OWASP file-upload guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/File_Upload_Cheat_Sheet.html). These support the backup/scanning approach; the proposed durations are operator choices, not statements of law.
